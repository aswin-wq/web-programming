Recipe for Momo

What You Need

  • Flour
  • Cabbage
  • Carrot
  • Onion
  • Ginger
  • Chili
  • Water

Steps

  1. Wash the vegetables like cabbage, carrot, onion, ginger, and chili thoroughly.
  2. Cut all the vegetables into small pieces so they cook evenly.
  3. Prepare the dough by mixing flour with water and kneading it until smooth.
  4. Roll the dough into small round pieces and fill them with the chopped vegetables.
  5. Make sure the momo is sealed well, or it may open while steaming.
  6. Place the momos in a steamer and cook until they are soft and fully done.
  7. Serve hot with chili sauce or spicy dip for the best taste.
